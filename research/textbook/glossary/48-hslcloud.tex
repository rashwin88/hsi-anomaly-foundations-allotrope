\glossentry{Cirrus}{Thin, high ice cloud; it adds a little brightness to the surface beneath and is classically detected at 1.38 micrometres, a band EnMAP L2A blanks.}
\glossentry{Cross-validation}{Splitting the data into k parts and testing each part with a model trained on the others, so every sample is tested once; grouped cross-validation keeps whole groups (here EnMAP datatakes) together.}
\glossentry{Datatake}{One satellite overpass; its tiles share atmosphere and sun angle.}
\glossentry{Recall}{For a class, the share of the pixels labelled with it that the classifier also calls it: diagonal entry over row total.}
\glossentry{Precision}{For a class, the share of the pixels the classifier calls it that are labelled with it: diagonal entry over column total.}
