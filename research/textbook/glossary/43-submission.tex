\glossentry{Prep bundle}{The export of a committed anomaly\_detection\_prep: a 0/1 mask GeoTIFF, a point shapefile without a score attribute, a CSV and a manifest, named hyper\_ or thermal\_ by sensor; the only bundle fit for submission.}
\glossentry{Product grid (PRISMA)}{The UTM grid of a PRISMA L2D product, read from its EPSG code and the corner pixels' centres: pitch = span / (n - 1), origin = upper-left centre minus half a pixel.}
\glossentry{Pixel centre}{The middle of a pixel, half a pixel in from its edges; PRISMA's corner attributes give the corner pixels' centres.}
\glossentry{Connectivity (4 and 8)}{Whether pixels that touch only at a corner count as neighbours (8) or not (4); the object table uses 8.}
\glossentry{Heatmap rule}{The evaluation rule that a continuous score map, or a score attribute that reads as one, disqualifies a submission.}
