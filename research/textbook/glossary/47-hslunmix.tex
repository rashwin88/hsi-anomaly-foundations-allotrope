\glossentry{Linear mixing model}{A pixel written as a weighted sum of pure spectra (endmembers) plus noise, x = E a + n, with non-negative abundances summing to 1.}
\glossentry{Endmember}{One of the pure spectra in the linear mixing model.}
\glossentry{Abundance}{The share of a pixel one endmember covers; for a target, its fill.}
\glossentry{Blind unmixing}{Finding both the endmembers and the abundances from the image alone.}
\glossentry{VCA}{Vertex component analysis: finds endmembers as the most extreme pixels of the data cloud.}
\glossentry{FCLS}{Fully constrained least squares: per pixel, the abundances that best fit the mixing model with both constraints.}
\glossentry{mSAD}{Mean spectral angle, in radians, between matched found and true endmembers.}
\glossentry{mRMSE}{Mean root-mean-square error of the abundance maps after matching.}
\glossentry{Object AUC}{The share of background pixels an object's best-scoring pixel outranks.}
\glossentry{Total variation}{The sum over side-by-side pixel pairs of the absolute difference of their values; as a penalty it shrinks every target by an amount proportional to perimeter over area.}
\glossentry{R-theta branch}{The unconstrained convolutional path in the HyperSL unmixing paper's reconstruction; it carried 29-56 percent of the reconstruction energy.}
