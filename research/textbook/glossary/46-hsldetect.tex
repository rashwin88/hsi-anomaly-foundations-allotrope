\glossentry{Spatial completion}{Predicting a pixel's spectrum from other pixels, its neighbours; how Indradhanu reconstructs a hidden pixel.}
\glossentry{Spectral self-completion}{Predicting some of a pixel's bands from its other bands; it rebuilds an anomaly's broad shape from the anomaly itself, which is why it is rejected for detection models here.}
\glossentry{Cloze test}{A reading test in which missing words of a sentence are filled in from the rest of the sentence; the analogy for spectral self-completion.}
